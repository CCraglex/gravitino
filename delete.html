Data Deletion

Game: Gravitino

How to Delete Your Data

You can permanently delete all your game data at any time directly from within the game.

Open Gravitino and use the trash can icon in the main menu to delete your data.

Before deletion, you will be asked to confirm:

“This will permanently delete your local and cloud save data. This action cannot be undone. Are you sure you want to continue?”

What Will Be Deleted

  • Local save data stored on your device
  • Cloud save data linked to Google Play Games

What May Be Retained

  • Purchase records (required by Google Play for legal and accounting purposes)
  • Anonymous crash reports or analytics data (if collected by third-party services)

Some data may be retained by third-party services as required by applicable laws.

Where Deletion Happens

All deletion is performed directly inside the game. No external request or email is required.
