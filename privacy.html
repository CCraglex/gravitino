Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 4, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravitino, accessible at https://ccraglex.github.io/gravitino/, collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our game.

This policy applies to all users of Gravitino worldwide.

Scope of This Policy

This policy describes how we handle data when you use Gravitino. It does not cover third-party services such as Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, or Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Information We Collect

  • Google account identifier (used for login and cloud save)
  • Game progress data (saved locally and in cloud storage)
  • Purchase information (handled by Google Play Billing)
  • Advertising identifier (used for showing ads)
  • Device information (device type, OS version, technical data)
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve gameplay experience
  • To save and synchronize game progress across devices
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To display advertisements
  • To analyze performance and fix bugs

How We Share Your Information

We may share limited data with the following services:

  • Google Play Games Services (authentication and cloud saves)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) (advertising services)
  • Service providers that help operate the game (e.g. cloud storage)

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and update your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising (via device settings)

For any privacy-related requests, contact: sanlikaaneren@gmail.com

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Applicable Laws

We aim to comply with applicable data protection laws such as GDPR and CCPA where required.
